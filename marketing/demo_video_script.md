# FastWrite — Demo Video Script (draft v1)

Purpose: the demo video required for Xero App Store certification. Target length **~2:30–3:00**. Shows the full flow: connect → upload → AI extract → review → push DRAFT → approve in Xero. Record at 1920×1080, app maximised, clean demo data, English UI, with the dev license chip hidden or cropped.

Format below: **[timecode] ON-SCREEN — VOICEOVER.**

---

### 0:00–0:15 — Intro
**ON-SCREEN:** FastWrite logo, then the Dashboard.
**VO:** "This is FastWrite — an AI document extractor that turns your invoices and receipts into Xero bills, without the manual typing. Let me show you how it works."

### 0:15–0:35 — Connect to Xero
**ON-SCREEN:** Settings page → click "Connect to Xero" → Xero login/consent → back to the app showing green "Connected to [Organisation]".
**VO:** "FastWrite connects securely to your Xero organisation using OAuth. No passwords are stored in the app, and you can disconnect any time."

### 0:35–1:00 — Upload an invoice
**ON-SCREEN:** Upload & Extract → drag in a PDF → click "Register Documents" → confirmation.
**VO:** "Start by uploading an invoice — or a batch of them. FastWrite automatically detects each invoice and its supplier."

### 1:00–1:45 — AI extraction & review (the core)
**ON-SCREEN:** Open the document on the Approval screen with Tour Mode ON. Step through a couple of fields (e.g. Buyer Name, Invoice No.) highlighted on the invoice, then the line-items table.
**VO:** "Using AI, FastWrite reads the supplier, dates, totals, and every line item. It walks you through each field, highlighted on the original document, so you can confirm everything is correct. You're always in control of the data before it goes anywhere."

### 1:45–2:15 — Push to Xero as DRAFT
**ON-SCREEN:** Click the Xero push button → the "Push to Xero (as DRAFT)" modal → pick an account code → click "Push as DRAFT".
**VO:** "When you're happy, push it to Xero. Choose the account code, and FastWrite creates the bill — always as a DRAFT. It never approves or pays anything for you."

### 2:15–2:45 — Result in Xero
**ON-SCREEN:** Switch to Xero in the browser → Bills to pay → Drafts → open the new draft bill showing line items, supplier, and totals.
**VO:** "Here's the draft bill inside Xero — supplier, line items, and totals, ready for your final review. You check it and authorise it in Xero, just like any other bill."

### 2:45–3:00 — Outro
**ON-SCREEN:** Back to FastWrite; show the Settings privacy note / BYOK; end on logo + fastwrite.tech.
**VO:** "Your documents stay private on your own computer, and you use your own AI key. FastWrite — less typing, more control. Learn more at fastwrite.tech."

---

## Production notes
- Keep cursor movements slow and deliberate; pause ~1s on each key screen.
- Use clean demo data only (no real client invoices).
- Hide the desktop license chip (bottom-right) before recording, or crop it in post.
- Captions/subtitles recommended (accessibility + silent autoplay).
- Use the same supplier/invoice across the whole video so totals stay consistent between the push modal and the Xero draft (avoid mismatched numbers on screen).
- Optional: add a short on-screen label "DRAFT — you approve in Xero" when the bill is pushed, to reinforce the safety message Xero reviewers look for.
