v68: fix - the consent checkbox never appeared, and detail in the referral list

THE BUG (found by Stavros on the live v67, reproduced in a clean browser)
The consent checkbox was in the DOM but its box stayed hidden. Cause: the v67
hook went into render(id), which is called ONLY by menu navigation. The email
screen is reached through show('s-email'), which has no hooks. So the checkbox
never appeared for anyone - silently, with no console error.

My mistake: I assumed where the hook block lived instead of measuring it. The
codebase has two functions - show(id) flips the screens, render(id) fills them
- and only the second carries the hooks.

The hook now lives in show(), the single point every path goes through. Same
reasoning as the v49 lock comment three lines below it: what must hold on every
route belongs where every route passes, not on the buttons.

Reproduction, measured before and after: fresh profile, localStorage cleared,
service worker unregistered, open with ?ref= -> km_source = "ref:MH8Y9QVNHH"
correct, s-email shown, #ref-consent hidden:true. That is the failure.

DETAIL IN THE LIST (asked for by Stavros: date / time / day)
Granted where it is safe, refused where it is not:
- WITH consent: day, date and time. The email is already shown, so the time
  adds no exposure and helps the referrer match the invitation.
- WITHOUT consent: date ONLY. On an anonymous row the exact time identifies:
  "Εγγραφή #2 — 19/09 19:42" and a referrer who sent one invitation at 19:40
  knows exactly who it is. That would cancel, in practice, the refusal the
  person just gave us - the whole point of asking.
The split lives in SQL, next to the email guard: CASE WHEN ref_share = 1 THEN
created ELSE substr(created, 1, 10).
