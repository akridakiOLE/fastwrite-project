v69: paging on the referral list - "show N of M" and "N more"

ASKED FOR by Stavros: an arrow to open the full table, and to choose how many
rows at a time (5/10/20/50/200).

- GET /api/km/ref?n=<1..200>&off=<k>. n is clamped to 200 and off to >= 0: a
  phone on 4G must never be handed 300 rows because a parameter said so.
- the response carries off and lim, so the app knows whether more exist without
  a second call; `signups` stays the TRUE total.
- the button says how many are LEFT ("N more"), and disappears at zero. A bare
  "more" is a button pressed blind.
- "Showing N of M" under the list - the same pattern the Control Panel already
  uses, so the product behaves consistently.
- changing the page size restarts from the top: otherwise pages of two sizes
  interleave and rows appear twice.
