PART A — KOSTOMETRO

A1. What stays only on your phone

The invoices, photos, amounts, suppliers, your 12 words and the Gemini key (if you add one) are stored in the app's storage on your phone (in the web version: the browser's storage).

  • Without this storage the app does not work, and it is not used for anything else.
  • We have no access to them. The only exception is reading by Kostas, our artificial-intelligence assistant, if you turn it on (A9): then the photo passes through our server only for the moment of reading and is not stored.
  • The app does not encrypt this storage inside the phone. It is protected by the screen lock and by the phone's own encryption. Set a screen lock.

A1b. The app from the App Store and Google Play

  • Download and updates: these happen through Apple or Google, with your own account there and under their own terms. We do not learn who downloaded the app. The stores show us only aggregate statistics (e.g. number of downloads per country) and, if you have allowed it in your phone's settings, crash reports without your name.
  • No in-app purchases: the app does not sell anything and does not ask for payment details.
  • Camera: the app asks your phone for camera permission, and uses it only when you tap to photograph an invoice or to scan a QR code. You can revoke it in your phone's settings.
  • QR code scanning: the code is read on your phone, with Google's ML Kit tool. On Android, ML Kit sends Google technical diagnostics about its operation (the device's manufacturer, model and operating-system version, the app's name and version, response times). The camera image and the content of the code are not included in this data, according to what Google publishes. On iPhone the code is read with the tools of iOS itself (Apple): no third-party tool is used and the app sends no diagnostics for the scan.
  • Sign-in with Google, Microsoft or Apple: the sign-in screen opens in your phone's browser and then you return to the app. What A2 says applies.

A3. The sealed folder — what we can and cannot do

  • The folder is locked on your device with a random key. That key can be unlocked only by your 12 words.
  • We do not have the words, so we cannot open the folder, even if someone asks us to.
  • We see only that it exists, how large it is and when it changed.
  • It lives on Cloudflare servers with an EU data-storage commitment.
  • Because we keep your email next to it, we treat it as personal data, even though we cannot read it.

A4. The 12 words

They are the key to your account.

  • We do not have them and we cannot give them to you.
  • If you lose them together with your phone, the data in the folder can never be opened again.
  • To change them, you confirm it is you in two ways together: your device lock (fingerprint, face or PIN) and your account email (a 6-digit code we send you, or sign-in with Google/Microsoft/Apple). The change can also be made from a device that has been deactivated on your account, so that you can take your account back if someone got in with your words.
  • We send you a notification email when the words change and when another device joins your account with them. No email contains the words.
  • Moving to a new device with a QR code: the old device locks your account details (including your Gemini key, if you have added one) with a key that exists only inside the QR code. Only the locked package reaches us, and we cannot open it. The new device receives it only after you confirm your account email, and we then delete it; if it is not collected, it expires in 10 minutes and is deleted completely within 24 hours. We send you a notification email for every transfer. You move from the web version to the store app in the same way.

A5. Reading amounts with Gemini (Google)

If you add your own Gemini key, the invoice photo goes directly from your phone to Google. It does not pass through us, and the relationship for this transmission is between you and Google. According to Google's terms (last updated 28/4/2026):

  • If you are in the EEA, Switzerland or the United Kingdom: Google does not use the photos and responses to improve its products, even with a free key.
  • If you are elsewhere and your key is free: Google may use them to improve its products, and humans may read them.
  • In any case: Google keeps them for a limited time, for abuse monitoring, and may store them temporarily in any country.

Without a key, no photo is sent to Google. While you are using Kostas's gift (A9), the reading is done by Kostas and not by Google, even if you have added a key.

A6. Account deletion

From Settings → “Delete account” (Ρυθμίσεις → «Διαγραφή λογαριασμού»). It does not happen immediately: it happens after 72 hours. The reason: if someone is holding your unlocked phone, they could request deletion in your name. The 72 hours give you time to stop it. If you had signed in with Apple, on deletion we also cancel the sign-in with Apple (A2).

Without the app (e.g. if you have already uninstalled it): write to support@fastwrite.tech, from your account email, with the subject «Διαγραφή λογαριασμού Kostometro» (Kostometro account deletion). We will send you a 6-digit code to the same email to confirm that it is you, and the deletion then follows the same rules (72 hours, same emails, same results).

  • As soon as you request it, the account is frozen on all devices — nothing is uploaded, nothing is downloaded — and we send you an email stating the exact time it will be completed.
  • You can stop it, as long as the 72 hours have not passed, with your 12 words, from any device: inside the app or on the page fastwrite.tech/akyrosi.
  • We do not put a cancellation link in the email, on purpose. The link would live on the same phone that may have been lost or stolen. The 12 words are the only thing that whoever holds your device does not have.
  • After the 72 hours we delete: the sealed folder, the photos, the 12-word lock, the link to each of your devices, your email, the account's support requests and the access code. From then on there is no restore — neither with the 12 words, nor by us.
  • Your email is kept until the 72 hours expire (and no longer): we need it to send you the final notification. It is deleted together with everything else.
  • We send you up to three emails: when the deletion is requested · if you cancel it · and when it is completed. None contains your words or a link that executes anything.
  • One statistics row remains in our database with exactly four items: month of sign-up, month of deletion, country, where you came from. Next to them remains a random identifier, which we cannot link to you. It serves so that the same 12 words are never used again and so that your other devices learn that the account was deleted. With few users, four such items do not guarantee full anonymity, which is why we state them explicitly.
  • Your other devices keep the invoices they already have, until you delete them yourself with “Start fresh” («Ξεκίνα καθαρά»).
  • Backups: see section 7. Whatever is deleted may remain in our database's automatic restore points for up to 30 days more, and is then lost from there too.
  • Uninstalling the app from your phone does not delete the account; it deletes only what was on the phone itself.

A7. Support, “Your feedback” and our correspondence

  • Support from the app (☰ Menu → Support): each request gets a number (e.g. KM-XXXX-3). Your text, our replies and the status of the request are stored on our server, so that you can see the history inside the app and do not have to explain again from the beginning. The app version and your device type are recorded with it. The email address is not copied into the request; it is read from your account at the moment we reply. If you write an email to support@fastwrite.tech, you get an automatic reply with a number (KM-E-…) and the request is stored in the same way, together with the address you wrote from. Numbers that were issued but for which no message ever arrived are deleted after 24 hours.
  • How we read support@: the mailbox runs on Google Workspace. Messages are also forwarded to a personal Gmail mailbox of the controller, from which he replies. Replies are always sent from support@fastwrite.tech.
  • Correspondence log: for every email we exchange with you from support@ or stavros@fastwrite.tech, we keep a record on our server: the address and the name shown in the email, whether it came in or went out, the date, the subject, the first 200 characters of the text and a link to the conversation in our mailbox. The full text stays only in the mailbox. Purpose: so that no message is left unanswered. Legal basis: legitimate interest, 6(1)(f). Retention: 24 months.
  • Measuring opens and clicks — only if you accept it: if you tick the separate box “Measure whether I read the reply” («Να μετράτε αν διάβασα την απάντηση») in Support, then when we reply to you by email we measure whether you opened the message and whether you clicked a link, using the Mailtrack tool of MAILSUITE S.L. (Barcelona, Spain), which acts as a processor for us. The date and time of each open and click are recorded and, for clicks, your IP address, browser and operating system. Purpose: to know that our reply arrived, so that we do not contact you again unnecessarily, or can follow up with you if it got lost. Without the box we measure nothing in emails to you. Legal basis: consent, 6(1)(a); you can withdraw it at any time, by unticking the box or by writing to us “no tracking”.
  • Your feedback: we store the stars, the text, the app version, the month and the country. Your email only if you tick “I want a reply” («Θέλω απάντηση»). None of this is linked to your account. Legal basis: legitimate interest (6(1)(f)) for improving the service, and for the email your own choice.

A8. The assistant (artificial intelligence)

Inside Kostometro you may see the “💬 Help” («💬 Βοήθεια») button. It is an artificial-intelligence assistant, not a human, and it says so before you start. It helps with installation and account creation; once you complete sign-up, it is removed from your device — unless you ask to see it again (below).

  • What happens to what you write: your messages and the assistant's replies are stored on our server and processed on our behalf by Anthropic PBC (USA), which provides the artificial-intelligence model. Anthropic does not use the messages to train its models, according to its commercial terms.
  • Screenshots: if you send one, it is used only for that reply and is not stored — neither by us, nor after the reply. Only the note “[image]” remains in the history.
  • What it does not accept: messages that look like the 12 words or like a key (Gemini or other) are stopped on your device and on the server before they are stored or sent to the provider. Never send your 12 words, here or anywhere else.
  • Your email is requested and stored only if you ask for help from a human. A support request (A7) is then opened with the whole conversation, and we reply to you by email.
  • Follow-up appointment (only if you ask for it): at the end of the installation the assistant may ask you whether you want to talk again in a few days. If you say “yes”, we keep the date and your consent, and on that day we send you one email, to your account email, with a link that reopens the assistant for one conversation; the link expires after 14 days. You can also ask for the assistant again yourself from “Support”. If you say “no”, we send nothing. Legal basis: your consent, 6(1)(a). The record of the consent is kept for 24 months.
  • What the assistant sees about you: which sign-up step your device is at (A2), so that it does not ask you again. It does not see your invoices, the folder or your key, and it cannot change anything in your account.
  • Limits: to prevent abuse, we keep for each device (as a hash, without email) how many messages it sent. The assistant stops after a message limit and refers you to support.
  • How long we keep the conversation: 90 days from the last message; if you asked for a human, 24 months, like every support request. The device hash with the counter: 24 months.
  • Legal basis: your consent (6(1)(a)), which you give on the assistant's first screen. If you do not give it, the assistant does not open and nothing is sent.

A9. Invoice reading by Kostas (gift of 20 readings)

On the photo screen you can tap “Turn on Kostas AI” («Ενεργοποίηση Κώστα AI»). From that moment, up to 20 invoices that you photograph are read by Kostas, without a key and at no charge to you. Before you turn it on we tell you so on a screen, and without your own tap nothing is sent.

  • What is sent: the invoice photo (reduced, up to 1568 pixels), only for invoices you photograph after turning it on. Older invoices are not sent.
  • Where it passes through: from our server at Cloudflare to Anthropic PBC (USA), which reads the date, net amount, VAT and total and returns them to us. The amounts go back to your phone and you confirm them yourself.
  • What we store: neither the photo nor the amounts. The photo exists on our server only while the reading lasts (a few seconds) and is not written to a database, a file or a log. We keep only when you turned on the gift and how many readings you used (A2), and in total per day how many readings were made and how much they cost, not linked to any person.
  • What Anthropic does: according to its commercial terms, it does not use the photos to train its models and deletes them automatically within 30 days. Only if a request is automatically flagged for violating its usage rules may it keep it for up to 2 years.
  • Invoice photos contain third-party data (e.g. the supplier's name and VAT number). For these we act as processor, on behalf of your business, as for the sealed folder (A2), and Anthropic as sub-processor (section 3).
  • If no amount is read or if something fails, the reading does not count towards the 20.
  • After the 20: you continue to enter the amounts yourself, or you add your own Gemini key (A5). Nothing else is sent to Kostas.
  • Legal basis: contract (6(1)(b)) — the reading takes place because you requested it by turning it on.

  • C3 · Form on Facebook:
  • When you fill in our form inside Facebook, Meta is an independent controller for its platform. We receive your name, your email address and your answer to the form's one question.

Legal basis: legitimate interest, 6(1)(f). (b) to send you the app when it is ready, if you left your email. Legal basis: your consent, 6(1)(a).

6. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent (Articles 15–22).

  • How to exercise them: at support@fastwrite.tech. In Kostometro, deletion can also be done inside the app (A6), and without the app by email (A6), and for the email list with the link at the end of every message (C4).
  • Response: within one month.
  • Note: for the invoices in the folder we cannot give access or correct them ourselves, because we cannot open them. You see and correct them yourself in the app.
  • Automated decisions: we make none. The assistant (A8) answers questions; it decides nothing about you and does no profiling.
  • Complaint: you can lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (Cyprus) or the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Greece).

7. Security, backups and breaches

  • Measures: encryption of the folder on the device with a random key · the 12 words never leave the device · codes and temporary keys only as irreversible hashes · HTTPS connection · no IP in our databases.
  • Backups: our database at Cloudflare automatically keeps restore points for 30 days, in case of failure. That is why whatever is deleted may remain there for up to 30 days more. The sealed folders (R2) currently have no separate backup; when they get one, it will be stated here, with the same 30-day rule.
  • Breach: if a breach occurs, we notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours (Article 33). We notify you without undue delay when the breach poses a high risk to you (Article 34).

8. Minors

Our products are business tools and are not intended for persons under 18.

9. Changes

For every material change we inform you by email or inside the app 30 days in advance. A material change is one that changes what data we collect, why, to whom we give it, how long we keep it, or your rights — e.g. a new recipient, measuring opens in your emails, a change of retention period. Bug fixes, new buttons or screens, speed improvements and clarifications of wording are not material: they change only the date of the policy.
