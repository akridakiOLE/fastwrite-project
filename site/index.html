How it works

Four steps from PDF to Xero.

Upload invoices

Drag-and-drop one or many PDFs / images into the desktop app.

AI extracts data

Google Gemini reads supplier, invoice number, dates, totals, and line items based on your template.

Review & approve

Validate the extracted data in the side-by-side approval view. Edit anything that looks off.

Push to Xero

One click sends the invoice as a DRAFT Bill into your Xero organisation. You authorise it in Xero.
