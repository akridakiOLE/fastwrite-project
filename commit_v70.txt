BOTH DECIDED BY STAVROS (19/9/2026), the second over a recorded objection.

1. CONSENT WORDING. The checkbox now reads "Να ξέρει ποιον ευχαριστεί." -
   same meaning, positive framing, and it should raise opt-in honestly. The
   box stays UNCHECKED by default: that was never in question.

2. DAY / DATE / TIME ON EVERY ROW, including anonymous ones.
   My objection, made twice and logged to A320: on an anonymous row the exact
   time narrows down who it is, so a referrer who sent one invitation at 19:40
   and sees "19:42" identifies someone who declined to be named.
   Stavros decided otherwise. It stands on two things that are true here:
   the checkbox is a real choice (not pre-ticked), and the moment of signup is
   now STATED OUTRIGHT in the consent text, so nobody is surprised.

   THE CONSEQUENCE I FIXED WITHOUT BEING ASKED: the consent text still said
   "otherwise they see Εγγραφή #2 — <date>". With the time now always shown,
   that sentence had become false. A consent notice that understates what we
   display is worse than no notice at all. It now says the day and time are
   visible either way, and that the box only controls the email.

WHAT DID NOT CHANGE, AND IS NOT NEGOTIABLE: the email never leaves the
database without ref_share = 1, and that guard lives in SQL.
